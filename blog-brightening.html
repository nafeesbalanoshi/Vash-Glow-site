Dull & Uneven Skin: How to Restore Radiance

Vash Glow Dermaceuticals · Vash Glow Journal

Dull, uneven-looking skin is one of the most common complaints — and it's rarely just about being tired. There are specific, treatable reasons skin loses its natural radiance.

What causes dullness and unevenness

A buildup of dead skin cells on the surface reflects light poorly, making skin look flat and tired. Combined with uneven melanin distribution — from old acne marks, sun exposure or hormonal changes — skin can look patchy and inconsistent in tone, even without obvious dark spots.

Why cleansing and brightening work together

A brightening routine works best in two steps: a cleanser that gently clears surface buildup so skin reflects light evenly, and a treatment cream that targets melanin production underneath to even out tone over time. Using only one of the two gives partial results.

How the Vash Glow routine helps

The Advanced Brightening Face Wash clears buildup and dullness at the surface with alpha arbutin and niacinamide, while the Skin Brightening Cream works underneath with glutathione and vitamin C to reduce pigmentation and even out tone. Used together, morning and night, they address both layers of what causes skin to look dull and uneven.
