Hair Fall

Why Hair Falls Out — And What Actually Helps

Vash Glow Dermaceuticals · Skincare Journal

Some hair shedding is completely normal — most people lose 50–100 hairs a day. But when hair fall becomes noticeable, it's usually a combination of a few underlying factors.

What causes excess hair fall

Common contributors include a weakened scalp environment (often from dandruff or fungal buildup), weak hair roots, nutritional gaps, and harsh shampoos that strip the scalp's natural balance. Dandruff in particular creates ongoing scalp irritation that can weaken hair roots over time if left untreated.

Why scalp health comes first

Hair grows from the scalp, so any routine aimed at reducing hair fall has to start there — controlling dandruff, soothing irritation, and strengthening the follicle environment — rather than only focusing on the hair strands themselves.
