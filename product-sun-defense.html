Product Details

An advanced broad-spectrum fluid screen that forms an invisible cellular shield against heavy UVA and UVB radiation. Infused with active antioxidants, it prevents sun-induced premature aging, blocks dark spot formation, and completely eliminates sun damage without leaving any white cast or heavy grease layer.

Core Actives: Advanced UVA/UVB Filters, Premium Niacinamide, and Vitamin E (Tocopheryl Acetate).

Base: Aqua, Ethylhexyl Methoxycinnamate, Zinc Oxide, Titanium Dioxide, Phenoxyethanol, Hyaluronic Acid core base. (Hypoallergenic & Oil-Free).

Apply generously to your face, neck, and all exposed skin areas 15–20 minutes before sun exposure as the final step of your skincare routine. Reapply every 3-4 hours if swimming, heavily sweating, or under prolonged direct sunlight.

Warnings

Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. Store below 30°C in a dry place, protected from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Suitable for: Suitable for all skin types, ideal for daily use, works well under makeup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it leave a white cast?

No — it's formulated to be lightweight and non-greasy with no white cast.

Can I wear makeup over it?

Yes, it's designed to work well under makeup.

How often should I reapply?

Every 2 hours of sun exposure, or after swimming or heavy sweating.
