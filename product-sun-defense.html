Full Description

Sun Defense Cream offers SPF 60 PA++++ broad-spectrum protection while keeping the skin-brightening ingredients — niacinamide, vitamin E and glutathione — working underneath. It's the daily step that protects the results of your brightening routine.

Benefits

  • Broad spectrum UVA/UVB protection
  • No white cast
  • Lightweight, non-greasy
  • Hypoallergenic, fragrance-free

Ingredients

Niacinamide, Vitamin E, Glutathione, and broad-spectrum sunblock actives.

Directions

Apply evenly to face and exposed skin 20–30 minutes before sun exposure. Reapply every 2 hours or as needed after swimming or excessive sweating.

Warnings

Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. Store below 30°C in a dry place, protected from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Suitable Skin Type

Suitable for all skin types, ideal for daily use, works well under makeup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it leave a white cast?

No — it's formulated to be lightweight and non-greasy with no white cast.

Can I wear makeup over it?

Yes, it's designed to work well under makeup.

How often should I reapply?

Every 2 hours of sun exposure, or after swimming or heavy sweating.
