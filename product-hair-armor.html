Full Description

Hair Armor Plus Shampoo combines biotin, procapil, bicapil, zinc pyrithione, climbazole and vitamin B5 in one advanced formula — built to reduce hair fall, control dandruff and leave hair stronger with regular use. Paraben, sulfate, silicone and cruelty free.

Benefits

  • Reduces hair fall

  • Nourishes deeply
  • For all hair types

Ingredients

Biotin, Procapil, Bicapil, Zinc Pyrithione, Climbazole, Vitamin B5. Paraben-free, sulfate-free, silicone-free, cruelty-free.

Directions

Apply to wet hair, massage gently into the scalp, leave for 2–3 minutes, then rinse thoroughly. Use 3–4 times a week for best results.

Warnings

Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
