Product Details

Glutafad Hand & Foot Whitening & Brightening Cream is designed for areas that commonly become dry, rough or uneven-looking, including hands, feet, elbows and knees. The formula combines moisturising ingredients with brightening-focused actives for a smoother, more even-looking appearance.

Core Actives: Urea, Niacinamide, Lactic Acid, Alpha Arbutin, Glutathione, Vitamin E

  1. Clean and dry the target area.
  2. Apply a small amount and massage gently.
  3. Use regularly according to the product label.

Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children. Patch test before first use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas is this cream for?

It is intended for hands, feet, elbows and knees, especially areas that feel dry, rough or uneven-looking.

How often should I use it?

Use it regularly according to the product label and your skin's tolerance.

Can I use it on my face?

The supplied packaging identifies it for hands, feet, elbows and knees. Use it only on the areas recommended on the label.
