Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Store below 30°C in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Do not use if you have known sensitivity to any of the ingredients.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I see results?

Most brightening routines show visible improvement in skin tone within 4–6 weeks of consistent, twice-daily use.

Can I use this with sunscreen?

Yes — in fact it's recommended. Apply Vash Glow Sun Defense Cream over it during the day to protect results.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It's dermatologically tested, but as with any active skincare product, we recommend a patch test first.
