Full Description

Vash Glow Skin Brightening Cream is built around two well-studied brightening actives — glutathione and vitamin C — working together to target dark spots, uneven tone and dullness. It's formulated for both men and women and designed to fit into a simple, twice-daily routine.

Benefits

  • Whitening & glow booster
  • Reduces pigmentation
  • Evens skin tone
  • Improves skin radiance

Ingredients

Glutathione, Vitamin C, and a dermatologically tested cream base suited for daily facial use.

Directions

Apply a small amount to clean, dry skin in the morning and evening. Gently massage until absorbed. Follow with sunscreen during the day.

Warnings

Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Store below 30°C in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Do not use if you have known sensitivity to any of the ingredients.

Suitable Skin Type

Suitable for all skin types, for men and women.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I see results?

Most brightening routines show visible improvement in skin tone within 4–6 weeks of consistent, twice-daily use.

Can I use this with sunscreen?

Yes — in fact it's recommended. Apply Vash Glow Sun Defense Cream over it during the day to protect results.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It's dermatologically tested, but as with any active skincare product, we recommend a patch test first.
