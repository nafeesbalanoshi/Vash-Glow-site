Full Description

ANAEXO is a next-generation hair serum with a powerful blend of active ingredients designed to support stronger, healthier and fuller-looking hair. Its advanced formula brings together AnaGain Pro, Procapil, Biotin, Blue Exosome and Coffee Bean Extract in a lightweight, leave-on serum.

Product Details

Engineered with high-performance active inhibitors to arrest hair fall at the roots. Procapil and AnaGain Pro work in perfect clinical synergy to stimulate hair bulb metabolism, expand the anagen growth phase, and delay hair thinning. It actively increases cellular density, reviving dormant follicles for stronger, visibly thicker strands.

Core Actives: AnaGain Pro (Organic Pea Sprout Extract), Procapil (Oleanolic Acid & Apigenin), Pure Biotin (Vitamin B7), Blue Exosome Extract, and High-Grade Coffee Bean Extract (Caffeine).

Base Formula: Aqua, Butylene Glycol, PPG-26-Buteth-26, PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil, Phenoxyethanol, Ethylhexylglycerin. (100% Fragrance-Free, Silicone-Free, Non-Greasy Formulation).

Apply 4-5 drops of ANAEXO Serum directly onto a clean, dry scalp, focusing primarily on areas with active hair thinning or receding lines. Gently massage into the scalp using your fingertips in upward circular motions for 2-3 minutes until fully absorbed. Do not rinse out. For optimal, accelerated results, use consistently every single night before bed.

Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Do a patch test before first use and discontinue if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
