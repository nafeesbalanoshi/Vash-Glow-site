Our Story

Skincare built for skin like ours.

Vash Glow Dermaceuticals started in Quetta with a simple idea: brightening and acne care formulated around actives that are named on the box, not hidden behind a marketing word like "glow."

Why Vash Glow exists

South Asian skin has its own set of common concerns — pigmentation, melasma, oiliness and acne that don't always get addressed by imported, generic skincare. Vash Glow formulates for those concerns specifically, using ingredients that are dermatologically tested and clearly listed.

Every product carries a batch number, manufacturing date and expiry date, the same way a pharmaceutical product would — because skincare that touches your face deserves that level of transparency.

How we formulate

Each formula is built around a small number of well-studied actives — glutathione and vitamin C for brightening, alpha arbutin and niacinamide for tone, salicylic and mandelic acid for acne, biotin and procapil for hair fall — rather than long ingredient lists designed to look impressive.
