Full Description

A daily face wash built to do more than cleanse — Advanced Brightening Face Wash pairs deep-cleansing agents with alpha arbutin, licorice extract, niacinamide and hyaluronic acid to brighten and hydrate skin with every wash.

Benefits

  • Deep cleanses
  • Brightens & refreshes skin
  • Hydrates & soothes
  • For all skin types

Ingredients

Alpha Arbutin, Licorice Extract, Niacinamide, Vitamin B12, Ethyl Ascorbic Acid, Hyaluronic Acid, Vitamin E, Aqua, and gentle cleansing agents.

Directions

Wet face with water, apply a small amount, massage gently in circular motions, then rinse thoroughly. Use morning and night. Follow with a moisturizer.

Warnings

Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Follow with a moisturizer to keep skin hydrated.

Suitable Skin Type

Suitable for all skin types, dermatologically tested.
